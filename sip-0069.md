[SIP-25](sip-0025.md) introduces two identities by telling each the address
the exchange observed the other arrive from. It pairs on identity alone, so
two parties that reached the exchange over different address families are
each handed an address they have no route to. This document makes the family
part of the match: a request is offered on the family it arrived over, a pair
is introduced only on a family both offered, a party reachable on more than
one offers each by asking over it, and a pair that shares none is told so
rather than being given an address that cannot be dialled.
